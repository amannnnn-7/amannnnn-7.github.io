baba is RL

January 2025 - May 2025 with Prof. Sandeep Manjanna

Training reinforcement learning agents to play the puzzle game "Baba is You", which features complex rule-based mechanics and requires abstract reasoning. This project explores how different RL architectures handle dynamic game rules and compositional reasoning.

key contributions:

  • Devised training levels, environment pipeline for training RL models on Baba is You
  • Proposed, engineered and tested a range of models including DQN, PPO and Hierarchical RL
  • Implemented custom reward shaping to encourage exploration and rule understanding

technologies:

Python, PyTorch, OpenAI Gym, Reinforcement Learning

music style transfer

August 2024 - December 2024 with Prof. Anupam Sobti

Developed deep generative models for transferring musical styles between audio tracks while preserving content. This project applies computer vision techniques (CycleGAN, VAE) to the audio domain for creative music generation.

key contributions:

  • Engineered novel generative models for music style transfer
  • Implemented CycleGANs for audio feature encoding and style transformation
  • Developed Variational Autoencoders to enable effective style mixing in audio
  • Experimented with mel-spectrogram representations and audio reconstruction techniques

technologies:

Python, PyTorch, Librosa, CycleGAN, VAE, Audio Processing

canDTI - Drug Target Interaction Prediction

January 2024 - May 2024 with Prof. Siddharth

Research project focused on predicting drug-target binding affinity using deep learning. This work aims to accelerate drug discovery by computationally identifying promising drug candidates for specific protein targets.

key contributions:

  • Performed research and literature review for current ML trends in drug target interaction
  • Developed and completed CNN encoding, preprocessing tools for drug-target sequence data
  • Proposed and developed CNN-RNN hybrid model for prediction of binding affinity values
  • Achieved competitive performance on benchmark DTI datasets

technologies:

Python, TensorFlow, BioPython, CNN, RNN, Sequence Analysis
